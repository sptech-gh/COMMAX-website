About COMMAX Healthcare

Empowering Lives Through Dignity, Safety & Independence

COMMAX Healthcare Solutions Ltd is an established, compassionate healthcare and domiciliary care provider headquarted in Telford. We are committed to enriching the lives of adults and young individuals needing everyday assistance, complex healthcare management, or specialized supported living.

We support individuals with learning disabilities, autism spectrum conditions, mental health needs, sensory impairments, and physical mobility challenges. Our dedicated care teams work hand-in-hand with service users, families, social workers, and NHS clinical multidisciplinary teams to ensure holistic, dignified outcomes.

Our Healthcare & Care Services

Home Support

Domiciliary Care

Personalized home visits helping you maintain your lifestyle, safety, and routines in your own cherished home.

  • Morning, afternoon & bedtime visits
  • Medication management & prompting
  • Meal planning, cooking & hydration
  • Housework & laundry support
24/7 One-to-One

24-Hour Live-in Care

A dedicated, compassionate caregiver residing with you or your loved one to provide round-the-clock companionship and support.

  • Full 24/7 dedicated one-to-one presence
  • Alternative to residential care homes
  • Overnight care & waking nights
  • Respite care for primary family carers
Independence

Supported Living

Enabling young people and adults with learning disabilities and autism to live fulfilling, independent lives in the community.

  • Life skills & budget management
  • Community access & social inclusion
  • Support with education & employment
  • Tenancy & household autonomy
Dignified Care

Personal & Hygiene Care

Sensitive, highly respectful assistance with daily bathing, dressing, continence management, and physical transfers.

  • Bathing, showering & oral hygiene
  • Dressing & personal grooming
  • Continence & catheter care support
  • Safe hoist & mobility transfers
Healthcare Staffing

Nursing Agency Staffing

Supplying fully qualified Registered General Nurses (RGNs), HCAs, and Support Workers to care homes, hospitals, and private clients.

  • Short-notice shift cover & long contracts
  • Rigorous clinical compliance checks
